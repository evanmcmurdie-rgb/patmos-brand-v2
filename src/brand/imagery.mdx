## Storytelling

Images should tell a clear and relevant story. They should show people in context, interacting with technology in a way that feels natural and purposeful. Focus on moments of use rather than staged situations. The subject should feel engaged, with a clear sense of intent or outcome.

Each image should communicate value without explanation: what is happening, why it matters, and how it connects to the user.

## Color tone

Imagery is our main source of color. Lighting, materials, and environments should create a natural and balanced color expression. Tones should feel soft, restrained, and realistic. Avoid overly saturated, artificial, or stylized color treatments.

Across a set of images, the overall tonality should feel cohesive. No single image or color should overpower the others.

## Lifestyle

Lifestyle imagery shows people in real situations, interacting with technology in everyday environments. The focus should be on authenticity and presence, not performance or staging. Images should communicate openness, capability, and human connection, reinforcing the idea that technology exists to support people.

## Detail

We use detail shots to present systems, products, and ideas with clarity and intention. They help explain how things work, reveal structure, and highlight important details. We aim to present every element clearly and honestly, so people can understand and trust it for what it is.

We mainly use this approach for infrastructure, hardware, and software, but it also works for concepts that are hard to show in real-world environments.
