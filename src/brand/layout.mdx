## Application

### Margins

Always calculate margins by dividing the format's shortest edge by 15. For example, an A4 format's margin is 14 mm: 210 mm / 15 = 14 mm.

<MarginCalculator />

### Grid

We use a 12-column grid as the foundation. It allows subdivisions into 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, or 12 columns depending on the format and content. Use only as many columns as needed to keep layouts clear and purposeful.

### Gutter

Gutters should be 1/3 of the margin width. For an A4 format with a 14 mm margin, the gutter is 14 mm / 3 ≈ 4.7 mm.
