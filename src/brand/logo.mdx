## Clear zone

To protect the logo from other visual elements, always surround it with a minimum amount of clear space, and keep other graphic elements out of that area. The height of the Icon sets the size of this exclusion zone.

<ClearZone />

## Minimum size

To keep the logo legible, never reduce it below its minimum size.

  sizes={[
    { kind: "icon", label: "Icon", digital: "50 px", print: "10 mm" },

  ]}

## Color

Black is the default color for our logo. Use the white version on darker backgrounds and images for contrast and clarity. Legibility always comes first.
