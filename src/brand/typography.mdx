Typography is a core part of the Patmos identity. It appears in everything we make, shapes how we communicate, and keeps our expression consistent, clear, and recognizable.

## Fallback font

When Graphik isn't available, use our secondary typeface, Arial, which comes installed on nearly every computer. Use it in programs like PowerPoint or Word, or when you send editable documents outside Patmos.

PowerPoint and Word aren't as precise with type settings, so use their default leading and tracking when you set Arial.

## Typesetting
